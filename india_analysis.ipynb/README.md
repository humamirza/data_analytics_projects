# 📊 Data Analyst Job Market Analysis — India 🇮🇳

## Overview

Welcome to my project analyzing the **Data Analyst job market in India**!  
This project was created to explore which skills are most in demand, which pay the best, and how they overlap — helping data professionals make smarter career decisions.

The data used was sourced from **Luke Barousse’s Python Course**, which provided detailed job listings containing information on job titles, salaries, and required skills.  
Using Python, I performed data cleaning, analysis, and visualization to uncover insights about the Indian data job landscape.

---

## 🧠 The Questions

Here are the key questions I aimed to answer:

1. What are the most in-demand skills for data analyst roles in India?
2. How are these skill trends changing over time?
3. Which skills and tools pay the highest salaries?
4. What are the most _optimal_ skills — those that are both high in demand and high in salary?

---

## 🔍 The Analysis

### 1️⃣ Most In-Demand Skills for Data Analysts

To identify the top skills, I analyzed the frequency of skill mentions in job postings across India.

#### 📈 Visualization

![Trending Top Skills for Data Analysts in India](4_india.ipynb/images/skills_trend.png)

#### 💡 Insights

- **SQL** remains the most in-demand skill throughout the year
- **Python** and **Excel** continue to be essential for analysts
- **Tableau** and **Power BI** maintain consistent demand for visualization roles
- Cloud and data engineering tools are gaining visibility

---

### 2️⃣ Highest Paid & Most In-Demand Skills

I compared salary data with skill frequency to understand which technologies offer the highest median salaries.

#### 📊 Visualization

![Highest Paid and Most In-Demand Skills for Data Analysts in India](4_india.ipynb/images/salary_analysis.png)

#### 💡 Insights

- **MongoDB**, **Databricks**, and **PySpark** stand out as top-paying skills
- **SQL**, **Python**, **Excel**, **Power BI**, and **Tableau** dominate job postings
- Cloud tools like **AWS** and **Azure** are steadily gaining traction

---

### 3️⃣ Optimal Skills — High Demand + High Pay

Finally, I visualized which skills strike the right balance between strong demand and high salary potential.

#### 📈 Visualization

![Most Optimal Skills for Data Analysts in India](4_india.ipynb/images/optimal_skills.png)

#### 💡 Insights

- **SQL** and **Python** have the strongest overall demand
- **Spark**, **Power BI**, and **Tableau** balance good salaries with high demand
- **MongoDB** leads in salary, though demand is more niche
- Cloud skills like **AWS** and **Azure** are great long-term investments
